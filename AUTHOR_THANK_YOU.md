# Thank you to the authors

Thank you to Giorgio Racca, Michal Valko, and Amartya Sanyal for making
*Language Generation with Replay: A Learning-Theoretic View of Model Collapse*
and its proof structure available. The explicit replay constructions and
versioned theorem mapping made a careful claim-by-claim audit possible.

This repository is an independent reproduction audit. It records scoped proof
evidence and limitations and does not imply review, endorsement, or approval by
the authors.
